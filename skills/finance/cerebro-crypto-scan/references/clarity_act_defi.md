# CLARITY Act (H.R.3633) — DeFi / Compound nexus

Condensed from congress.gov bill text + legal analyses (verified 2026-08).
For the scan, the only thing that matters: how the Act touches DeFi lending
protocols like Compound (COMP).

## What it is
- **H.R.3633 — Digital Asset Market Clarity Act**, 119th Congress (2025–2026).
- Sponsor: Rep. French Hill (R-AR). Introduced 2025-05-29.
- **Status (2026-08):** Passed House 294–134 (Roll no. 199, 2025-07-17).
  Reported in Senate with amendment 2026-06-01 (Calendar No. 423).
  **PENDING SENATE — NOT YET LAW.**
- **Effective:** 360 days after enactment (later of 360 days or 60 days after the
  final implementing rule where rulemaking is required).

## DeFi provisions (the part relevant to COMP)
- Defines **"decentralized finance trading protocol"** = a distributed-ledger
  system through which multiple participants can execute a financial transaction
  *without an intermediary*.
- **Directs the SEC** to use its existing exemptive authority and specifically to
  exempt certain decentralized-finance activities. Net effect: the *protocol
  software* is generally carved out while *centralized front-ends* stay in scope.

## Mapping to the scan
- **COMP (Compound):** decentralized on-chain lending / money-market protocol,
  governed by the COMP token. Squarely a "DeFi trading protocol" → potential
  regulatory tailwind if enacted. Treat as a **thesis, not a done deal** until the
  Senate passes and the President signs.
- **ACT (Act I: The AI Prophecy):** NO CLARITY nexus. Covered in the scan only for
  completeness (forced watchlist); its `clarity_act_relation` explicitly says
  "no direct nexus". Do NOT read its presence as a catalyst.
