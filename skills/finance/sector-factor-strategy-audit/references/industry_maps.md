BUY-AND-HOLD RANKING (growth satellite, NOT income core — see mandate pitfall):
  1. SK Hynix (SKHY) — best HBM positioning + cheapest valuation; Korea/currency risk.
  2. Micron (MU) — US-domiciled, momentum, geopolitical premium; pricier, smaller HBM.
  3. Samsung (SSNLF) — turnaround/AI-pivot story, lagging HBM qualification.

YIELD REALITY CHECK: Micron ~0.07% (raised 30% to $0.15/qtr — still trivial).
Samsung/SK Hynix pay low yields (~1-2% historically). These are NOT DRIP/income
holdings — they compound only on price if the AI memory supercycle holds, and can
fall 50%+ in a memory down-cycle. Size as satellite (~few %), not core.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE MANDATE: when Victor asks "best to buy and hold" for a
cyclical/AI name, flag the yield gap vs the income core (SCHD/VYM/O/PEP/KO) and
recommend satellite sizing. Do not rank it like an income ETF.
